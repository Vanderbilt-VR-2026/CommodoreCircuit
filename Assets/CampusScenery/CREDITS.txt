CampusScenery data credits
==========================

Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors. Licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Source file: CampusSource/campus.osm (exported from openstreetmap.org, bbox west -86.8095, south 36.1425,
east -86.8000, north 36.1495).

alumni_scenery.json is derived from that data: real building footprints, footpaths, Alumni Lawn / Library Lawn
outlines and bench positions. Building heights, windows, roofs, trees and lamp posts are generated by us and are
approximations, not survey data.

Put "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" in the game's credits screen.
